---
title: Migration guides
description: Guides for migrating between major versions of Sequelize Guard
---

Choose the guide for your existing installation. Back up application and policy data, rehearse on a restored copy, and stop policy writers during the migration and cutover. Do not use force-sync to upgrade an existing database.

- [Upgrading from 6.0.2 to 6.1](/docs/migration/upgrade-to-v6-1): copy policy into a distinct namespace, verify grants and revocation, and plan rollback.
- [Original migration to v6](/docs/migration/upgrade-to-v6): historical instructions for the earlier TypeScript rewrite.

For a new installation, follow [database migrations](/docs/guide/db-migrations).
